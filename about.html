We teach the parts that actually get used at work.

SkillNova started because too many students finish a degree without ever building something real. We fix that with small batches, live teaching and projects you can show an interviewer.

How this started

In 2023, three of us were taking evening classes for students who couldn't afford a coaching centre. Twelve learners, one borrowed classroom, a whiteboard that never wiped clean. Nine of those twelve were placed within a year.

The format worked because it was small and live. People could interrupt, ask the dumb question, and get an answer that same evening. Recorded video alone never did that.

SkillNova is that classroom, made bigger without losing the part that mattered. Every course still runs live, every batch still has a trainer who reads your code, and every session is recorded so you can watch it again at 2 AM before a test.

What we believe

  • You learn by building, not by watching someone else build.
  • A trainer who has shipped real work teaches better than a script.
  • Feedback within a day beats perfect feedback next month.
  • Price should not be the reason someone stays untrained.

    One substantial build that goes on your resume, reviewed line by line.
